How FindIt Works

Three simple steps to report and recover items on campus

1

Report the Item

Submit details including the specific campus location, category, date, and description. An optional photo helps others identify it.

2

Search & Verify

Browse recent listings or filter by building, category, and date. Compare details to confirm whether it is your item.

3

Claim & Update

Safely coordinate handover through the campus security desk or department office, then mark the report as Recovered.
